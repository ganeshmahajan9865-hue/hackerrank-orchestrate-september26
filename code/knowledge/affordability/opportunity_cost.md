# Evaluating Opportunity Cost and Priorities

Every financial commitment consumes liquidity that could otherwise safeguard against emergencies, fund high-priority goals, or avoid expensive borrowing.

### Key Considerations
- Financial Priorities Alignment: Discretionary expenses should take lower precedence than scheduled savings goals and emergency reserves.
- Irreversible Commitments: Paying cash upfront is irreversible; once deployed, liquidity cannot easily be retrieved if unexpected expenses occur.
- Total Value Assessment: When a requested expenditure strains the financial buffer, recommending against the purchase (`not_recommended` / `not_affordable`) prevents long-term balance erosion.
