# Strategic Value of Delayed Purchases (Wait Strategy)

When a purchase cannot be paid safely today, waiting for upcoming confirmed income or recurring debt roll-offs is often the safest and most cost-effective recommendation.

### When Waiting is Recommended
- Confirmed Future Inflows: The user has verified, scheduled salary or guaranteed receivables arriving within the forecast window.
- Eliminates Financing Costs: Waiting allows the user to pay in full later, bypassing interest charges, financing fees, and multi-month debt drag.
- Earliest Safe Date: The system identifies the exact chronological day when accumulated net cash flow safely accommodates the entire cost while keeping the emergency buffer intact.
