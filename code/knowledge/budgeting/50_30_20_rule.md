# The 50/30/20 Budgeting Principle

The 50/30/20 framework divides after-tax income into three essential categories:
- 50% for Needs: Essential living obligations including rent, utilities, basic groceries, healthcare, and debt minimums.
- 30% for Wants: Discretionary lifestyle spending such as dining out, recreation, travel, and personal upgrades.
- 20% for Savings & Debt Acceleration: Emergency reserves, investments, and paying down debt balances.

### Core Rule for Purchases
A major discretionary expenditure should never crowd out funds earmarked for monthly needs or minimum emergency liquidity. If an upfront payment threatens the 50% safety allocation for essential commitments over the next 90 days, the purchase must be deferred, split, or financed through affordable installments.
