# Differentiating Essential Commitments from Discretionary Spending

Financial sustainability requires a clear distinction between protected essentials and adjustable discretionary spending.

### Protected Categories
Categories such as rent, mortgage, basic utilities, mandatory insurance, and healthcare are strictly protected. They cannot be cancelled, stopped, or scaled down to accommodate new lifestyle purchases.

### Discretionary Categories
Expenditures on dining out, luxury subscriptions, electronics upgrades, and entertainment represent flexible lifestyle choices. These categories provide an available lever when an essential or high-priority goal requires short-term cash flow adjustments.
