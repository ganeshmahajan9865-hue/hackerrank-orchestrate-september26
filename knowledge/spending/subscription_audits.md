# Auditing Recurring Subscriptions and Digital Services

Recurring micro-transactions—such as streaming services, cloud storage, gaming memberships, and gym subscriptions—can silently erode available cash flow.

### Audit Strategy
- Flagging Low-Utility Charges: Periodic review of automated debits reveals services with low usage that are eligible for immediate cancellation.
- Impact on Debt Capacity: Reallocating \$30-\$50 in monthly recurring subscriptions creates equivalent headroom to service an affordable short-term installment plan without stressing daily liquidity.
