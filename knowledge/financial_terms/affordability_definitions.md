# Standard Affordability Status Classifications

The affordability framework classifies every purchase evaluation into one of four mutually exclusive statuses:

### 1. `affordable_now`
The entire purchase amount can be safely paid upfront in full on the request date while preserving all essential commitments and maintaining the required minimum balance floor throughout the entire 90-day simulation window.

### 2. `affordable_with_plan`
The purchase cannot be paid in full immediately, but can be safely completed through a structured installment plan, a compliant partial payment schedule, or by making permitted flexible spending reductions.

### 3. `affordable_later`
The purchase cannot be safely started or split today, but the user has confirmed future cash inflows arriving before a safe future date where paying in full will not compromise the minimum balance.

### 4. `not_affordable`
The request cannot be safely serviced upfront, with any supplied installment plan, or on any projected date within the 90-day forecast horizon without dangerously breaching the minimum balance floor.
