# Accounting for Unforeseen Expenses and Conservative Buffers

Real-world cash outflows frequently experience unpredictable friction: emergency medical needs, sudden car repairs, or variable utility spikes.

### Conservative Principles
- Variable Essential Estimation: Historical dining, grocery, and fuel expenditures are modeled conservatively.
- Headroom Preservation: Running balance down to the exact dollar of minimum balance is inherently fragile; maintaining headroom provides resilience against volatile cash shocks.
- Non-Cash Events: Unrealized investment holdings, crypto gains, or pending refunds do not constitute emergency cash reserves until fully settled and liquid in the user's primary checking account.
